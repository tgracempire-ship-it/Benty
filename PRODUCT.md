# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Vite + React (TypeScript).

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and prospective clients looking for an expert Civil & Structural Engineer.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Ebenezer's professional experience, project gallery, and engineering expertise. Success means visitors are impressed by his track record and contact him to hire him.

## Positioning
Expert execution, site management, structural planning, and quality assurance. A proven track record of structural integrity, precision drafting, and proactive site leadership.

## Operating Context
Professional engineering portfolio viewed mostly on desktop by hiring managers, or on mobile by clients.

## Capabilities and Constraints
It is a single-page scrolling portfolio. The contact form uses formsubmit.co to send emails directly to Benytecons@gmail.com without a backend.

## Brand Commitments
Name: EBENEZER. 
Palette: Teal (#0F3E4D), Gold (#EFA526), Slate, White.
Font: Inter.
Professional, clean, highly engineered look.

## Evidence on Hand
Professional Experience data, gallery images (Unsplash placeholders for now), list of expertise.

## Product Principles
1. Clean and professional.
2. Direct and accessible (contact form always easily reachable).
3. Showcase the work visually.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast between text and background. Fully responsive layout.
